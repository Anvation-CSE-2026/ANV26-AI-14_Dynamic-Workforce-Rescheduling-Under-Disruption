# Plant Shift & Safety Policy

## 1. Working hours

### 1.1 Daily limit
No operator may be scheduled for more than 10 hours in a working day.

### 1.2 Weekly limit
Operators must not work more than 48 hours in a calendar week.

## 2. Rest between shifts
Operators must receive at least 11 hours of rest between shifts, including when rotating between Shift A, B and C.

## 3. Overtime
Overtime is capped at 8 hours per operator per week. All overtime requires approval from the shift supervisor.

## 4. Machine safety
Only operators holding Safety Level 2 certification may run CNC machines on Line 1.

## 5. Line staffing
Line 1 must have at least 2 CNC operators on Shift A.

## 6. Rotation
Operators should not work more than 6 consecutive days.
