# Teacher Workload & Substitution Policy

## 1. Teaching load

### 1.1 Daily load
No teacher may be scheduled for more than 5 hours in a working day.

### 1.2 Weekly load
Teachers must not work more than 30 hours in a calendar week.

## 2. Extra periods
Overtime is not permitted; substitution must fit within the weekly teaching load.

## 3. Subject qualification
Only teachers qualified in the subject may substitute for exam-year (Grade 9 and above) classes.

## 4. Fair substitution
Substitution duties should be rotated so that no teacher covers more than 3 extra periods in a week.

## 5. Communication
The academic coordinator must inform class teachers of any substitution before the first period.
