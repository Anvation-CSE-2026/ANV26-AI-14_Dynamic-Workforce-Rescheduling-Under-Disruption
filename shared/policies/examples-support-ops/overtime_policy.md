# Overtime Policy

## 1. Approval
All overtime requires prior approval from the operations manager.

## 2. Limits
Overtime is capped at 8 hours per employee per week.

## 3. Distribution
Overtime opportunities should be offered fairly, rotating among qualified employees.

## 4. Compensation
Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the base hourly rate.
