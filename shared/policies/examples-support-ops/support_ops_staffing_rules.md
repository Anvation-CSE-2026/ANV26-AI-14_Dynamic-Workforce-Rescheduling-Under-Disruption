# Customer Support Operations Staffing Rules

## 1. Coverage

### 1.1 L2 Support queue
The L2 Support queue must have at least 3 agents on shift between 09:00 and 21:00.

### 1.2 Kannada language desk
At least 1 Kannada-speaking agent must be on shift during business hours.

## 2. Skills
Only agents certified in Tier-2 troubleshooting may cover the L2 Support queue.

## 3. Peak season hours
During peak season, L2 Support agents may work up to 45 hours per week.

## 4. Schedule stability
An agent's schedule should not be changed more than 3 times in a calendar month.

## 5. Preferences
Agent shift preferences should be respected where possible.

## 6. Notice of changes
Agents must be notified of any schedule change at least 12 hours before the affected shift.
