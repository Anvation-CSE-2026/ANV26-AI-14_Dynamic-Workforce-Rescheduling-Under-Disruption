# Working Time Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets limits on working time to protect employee health and to comply with applicable labour regulations. It applies to all employees on the operations roster.

## 2. Working hours

### 2.1 Daily hours
No employee may be scheduled for more than 9 hours in a working day, excluding unpaid breaks.

### 2.2 Weekly hours
Employees must not work more than 40 hours in a calendar week.

## 3. Rest and recovery

### 3.1 Rest between shifts
Employees must receive at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

### 3.2 Consecutive working days
No employee may work more than 6 consecutive days.

## 4. Weekend work
Employees should not be scheduled for more than 2 weekends per month.

## 5. Timesheets
Timesheets must be submitted by Friday of each week through the HR portal.
