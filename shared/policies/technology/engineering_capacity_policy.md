# Engineering Capacity & On-Call Policy

## 1. Working hours

### 1.1 Daily capacity
No engineer may be scheduled for more than 10 hours in a working day.

### 1.2 Weekly capacity
Engineers must not work more than 48 hours in a calendar week.

## 2. On-call rest
Engineers must have at least 10 hours of rest after an on-call shift before starting planned work.

## 3. Overtime
Overtime is capped at 8 hours per engineer per week. Overtime requires approval from the engineering manager.

## 4. On-call eligibility
Only engineers who are On-call Certified may take a primary on-call rotation.

## 5. Release work
Release-critical work should be covered by an engineer with Payments Domain experience.
