# Driver Hours & Dangerous Goods Policy

## 1. Driving hours

### 1.1 Daily limit
No driver may be scheduled for more than 9 hours in a working day.

### 1.2 Weekly limit
Drivers must not work more than 48 hours in a calendar week.

## 2. Rest
Drivers must receive at least 10 hours of rest between runs.

## 3. Overtime
Overtime is capped at 8 hours per driver per week. All overtime requires approval from the dispatcher.

## 4. Dangerous goods
Only drivers holding a Hazmat Permit may drive hazmat routes.

## 5. Customer windows
Dispatch should notify customers of any driver change on routes with a delivery window.
