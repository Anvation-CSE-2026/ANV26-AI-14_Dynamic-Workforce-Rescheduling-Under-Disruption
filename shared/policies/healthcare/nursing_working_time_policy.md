# Nursing Working Time & Staffing Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all registered nurses rostered in inpatient units of St. Aster Multispeciality Hospital.

## 2. Working hours

### 2.1 Shift length
No nurse may be scheduled for more than 12 hours in a working day.

### 2.2 Weekly limit
Nurses must not work more than 48 hours in a calendar week, including overtime.

## 3. Rest
Nurses must receive at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next.

## 4. Overtime

### 4.1 Overtime cap
Overtime is capped at 12 hours per nurse per week.

### 4.2 Approval
All overtime requires approval from the nurse manager.

## 5. Unit staffing

### 5.1 ICU certification
Only nurses certified in ACLS may be assigned to ICU shifts.

### 5.2 ICU minimum staffing
The ICU must have at least 2 nurses on every day shift.

## 6. Fair distribution
Short-notice cover should be shared fairly so that the same nurses are not repeatedly called in.
